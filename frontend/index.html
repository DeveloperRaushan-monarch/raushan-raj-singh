Forensic Interpretation

Different file hashes do not necessarily mean the visible images are different. Re-saving a JPEG, stripping EXIF metadata, or resizing dimensions completely alters the SHA-256 digest while preserving high visual similarity. Similarity does not establish common origin or authenticity.

About PIXELTRUTH

PIXELTRUTH is a local-first digital image-forensics workstation designed to inspect structural properties, embedded metadata, and compression indicators without transmitting evidence to external servers.

Local & Private Execution

All hashing, EXIF/XMP/IPTC parsing, ELA residual computation, histogram generation, assessment rules, and PDF generation run locally in memory. Uploaded files remain on your workstation and are never transmitted externally.

Gated Geolocation Privacy

Embedded GPS tags remain concealed until explicitly revealed in Metadata Explorer, and are excluded by default when generating PDF forensic reports.

Objective Indicators

PIXELTRUTH reports observable technical evidence — cryptographic digests, metadata statuses, and compression residuals — without making automated claims of authenticity or manipulation.

Monochrome Studio Standard

A strict black-and-white interface eliminates chromatic bias when inspecting tonal distributions, compression artifacts, and side-by-side visual comparisons.
