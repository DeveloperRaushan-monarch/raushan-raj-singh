ELA Explanation and Limitations

How It Works

Error Level Analysis resaves the image at a known JPEG quality and computes the per-pixel difference against the input. Areas that have undergone fewer compression cycles may exhibit brighter residuals than surrounding surfaces.

Analytical Limitations

High-contrast edges and textured surfaces naturally produce brighter ELA responses. Lossless formats (PNG/TIFF) or heavily re-saved JPEGs cannot be reliably evaluated with ELA alone.
